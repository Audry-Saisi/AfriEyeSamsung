AFRIEYE: WHAT TO DO WITH THESE FILES
Research demo, not a medical device. Don't enter real patient data.

1. COPY THE FILES INTO YOUR PROJECT
Unzip into a temporary folder, then copy everything into AfriEyeSamsung. In PowerShell:

  Expand-Archive "$HOME\Downloads\AfriEye-Update.zip" "$HOME\Downloads\AfriEye-Update" -Force
  Copy-Item "$HOME\Downloads\AfriEye-Update\*" "C:\Users\Administrator\Desktop\AfriEyeSamsung" -Recurse -Force

This adds new files. It doesn't change your notebooks 01 to 07 or their results.

2. RUN THE APP
In the VS Code terminal, inside AfriEyeSamsung, with .venv active:

  pip install -r requirements-app.txt
  python app/run.py

Open http://localhost:8200 and sign in:
  CHW:             0711 000 001   password demo-chw-2026
  Eye specialist:  0711 000 002   password demo-eye-2026
Stop it with Ctrl+C. To see the demo with no server, double-click app/dist/afrieye_demo.html.

3. RUN THE NOTEBOOKS IN THIS ORDER
  notebook 07     Fix the error first, and note the XGBoost result
  notebook 08     Upload to Google Colab. Needs a free Google account and T4 GPU. Unzip its results into AfriEyeSamsung.
  notebook 09     In VS Code: checks for camera bias
  notebook 10     In VS Code: chooses the final model
  notebook 11     In VS Code, once you have new photos: see docs/adapter_test_plan.md
  notebook 12     In VS Code: puts a newly chosen neural network into the app

WHAT HAS AND HASN'T BEEN TESTED
Tested here: notebooks 09, 10 and 11 on the real data, and the full app in an automated browser, including offline use.
Not run yet: notebooks 08 and 12 need PyTorch, which wasn't available where they were written, so expect small fixes.
Also not tested: loading an exported model in the app's browser, a real phone, a real SMS, and real adapter photos.
Offline start on a phone needs HTTPS. Over plain Wi-Fi, the phone can scan only while the page is already open.
